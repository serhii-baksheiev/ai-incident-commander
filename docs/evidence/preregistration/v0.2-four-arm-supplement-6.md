# Preregistration supplement 6: the deterministic prediction path

Written on 2026-09-28, before any paid run. Supplement 2 said the deterministic
prediction path would be registered in a further supplement before AIC-121
runs, and supplement 5 said the same for the path that evaluates the
observation table's facts. This is that supplement (AIC-124 slice c).

Every row of `v0.2-four-arm.md` and supplements 1 to 5 stands as registered,
except where a section below says what it adds to one.

## What is registered

- **The template table `prediction-templates-v1`.** One entry per
  `ROOT_CAUSE_MECHANISMS` mechanism, naming the mechanism only; the subject of
  every expected observation is the hypothesis's own `cause.component`. See
  prediction-nodes.test.mjs › "pins the exact template table content: version,
  and every mechanism, key, and observation list", › "PREDICTION_TEMPLATES.byMechanism
  carries exactly one key per ROOT_CAUSE_MECHANISMS entry, in both directions"
  and › "never names a corpus evidence id, replay scenario id, or
  OBSERVATION_ANNOTATIONS subject: a template commits to the mechanism only,
  and derivePredictions fills the subject in from the hypothesis".
- **The evaluation rules `prediction-evaluation-v1`.** No model call and no
  judge: a prediction is confirmed, refuted, untestable or left untested by
  rule, from typed facts only, and a decided prediction adds one
  rule-produced assessment per evidence item that decided it. The rules and
  their rows are listed in the header of
  `packages/domain/src/prediction-evaluation.ts`, each with its test.
- **The as-of instant `REPLAY_AS_OF`.** The lane evaluates at the instant the
  replay corpus was recorded. See prediction-wiring.test.mjs › "@aic/evals
  exports REPLAY_AS_OF as the exact instant the whole replay corpus was
  recorded", › "every evidence item in every ok fixture entry of every
  REPLAY_SCENARIOS scenario was observed at or before REPLAY_AS_OF" and ›
  "scriptedNodes(record) and modelNodes(record, port) carry the canonical
  derive_predictions and evaluate_predictions: a matching
  connection-pool-exhaustion fact observed at REPLAY_AS_OF confirms the
  derived prediction with one rule assessment, and the same fact one
  millisecond later confirms nothing".
- **Where predictions come from.** Only `derive_predictions` creates them: on
  its own edge after `generate_hypotheses`, and inside the challenge round for
  the alternative, before the round's `execute_investigation`. The evaluator
  never creates one. See prediction-nodes.test.mjs › "the evaluate node never
  derives predictions itself: a hypothesis with a cause and a matching fact but
  no predictions in state yields no predictions and no assessments" and
  prediction-wiring.test.mjs › "the challenge round derives predictions for the
  alternative, through createDerivePredictions, before the challenge edge
  reaches execute_investigation for the round — and leaves the leader's
  already-derived prediction unchanged".

## Which arms it reaches

- **Graph-model:** wired. See prediction-wiring.test.mjs › "through the real
  kernel, modelNodes(record, port) gives a model-proposed hypothesis a
  confirmed prediction and one rule assessment, and gives the challenge
  alternative its own predictions too".
- **Scripted-control:** wired, and it changes nothing, because its hypotheses
  carry no cause. See prediction-wiring.test.mjs › "running the real kernel
  over a calibration record with scriptedNodes ends with predictions: [] and no
  producedBy: "rule" assessment".

## What the model roles now see

Supplement 5 registered that no model sees the facts. That still holds. What
changes is that the graph arm's model roles now see what the rules derived
from the facts: each prediction with its status, and each rule-produced
assessment. See prediction-wiring.test.mjs › "a model role is shown the
derived prediction verdicts and rule assessments, never the observation facts
behind them".

The naive arm sees neither. This adds to confound 3 of `v0.2-four-arm.md`: the
two arms no longer differ only in presentation. The graph arm also receives
the deterministic verdicts its own prediction path computed from the reviewed
observation table. A graph advantage therefore includes that path. It is the
mechanism the comparison measures, and it is registered here so it is not read
as a same-information comparison.

## The registered expectation on calibration

Measured before any paid run, with each calibration scenario's own
ground-truth cause as the only hypothesis, over that scenario's replayed
evidence at `REPLAY_AS_OF`:

| scenario | confirmed | otherwise |
| --- | --- | --- |
| deployment-caused-incident-a | deployment before onset | error rate untested |
| challenge-keeps-leader | deployment before onset | error rate untested |
| bad-deployment | — | all untested |
| db-pool-exhaustion | — | all untested |
| dependency-caused-incident-b | — | all untested |
| transient-self-resolved | — | all untested |

No prediction is refuted in any row. `false-alert` and
`multiple-plausible-causes` have no ground-truth cause and are not evaluated.
See prediction-wiring.test.mjs › "the calibration outcome matrix: evaluating
each rootCause-carrying scenario's own root cause against its own replayed
evidence at REPLAY_AS_OF equals the registered literal matrix, and no
prediction is ever refuted" and › "BENCHMARK_SCENARIO_PARTITIONS.calibration
scenarios with no STRUCTURAL_GROUND_TRUTH rootCause are exactly false-alert and
multiple-plausible-causes".

So even the ground-truth cause can reach `supported` on at most two of the
eight calibration scenarios, because `supported` needs a confirmed prediction.
See prediction-nodes.test.mjs › "R': the same two supports without the
confirming fact (the prediction stays untested) yields corroborated, never
supported".

The deployment template is weakly discriminating. In
`dependency-caused-incident-b` it also confirms for a cause that is not the
root cause, because a deployment did land before onset there. See
prediction-wiring.test.mjs › "distractor: dependency-caused-incident-b
evaluated against a cause that is not its own root cause still confirms
deployment-before-onset, because the template only checks whether a
deployment landed pre-onset".

These are registered as found. The templates were not changed after this
measurement, and the hold-out scenarios were not evaluated for it.

## Candidate identity

The change touches `packages/` and `scripts/`, so it moves the candidate
fingerprint, as every behaviour change does. A calibration or hold-out run
admitted after this supplement runs this path.
