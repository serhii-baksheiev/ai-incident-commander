## The hold-out premise

The four-arm preregistration assumes an untouched hold-out. The
`docs/evidence/final-evaluation/` records say something narrower.

- **Four `complete` records did not reach a hold-out example.** Their model arms
  made 4, 3, 15 and 23 provider calls (`05714e89391a`, `b5a0121d72b6`,
  `872ef36dea33`, `cf68c16c8592`). The final-evaluation plan puts every
  calibration example before the first hold-out example:
  `packages/evals/src/benchmark-evaluation.ts` spreads
  `BENCHMARK_SCENARIO_PARTITIONS.calibration` ahead of `.holdout`. At three runs
  per scenario that is 24 calibration records. On those records' commits, each
  graph-model record's first reasoning node is a model role, so each record costs
  at least one call. 23 calls cannot reach record 25. The plan keeps the
  partition's scenario order and lists each scenario's runs together
  (`scenariosForPartition` and `buildBenchmarkPlan` in the same file).
- **Three `claimed` records state in their own `interruption` notes that the
  model executed against hold-out scenarios** (`145a2afba7b8`, `587842baeabb`,
  `6362d883decd`). None of them carries a usage figure, so the bound above
  cannot confirm or refute that statement.
- **No hold-out score was ever produced.** Every model arm in these records is
  unreportable.

This conflicts with `docs/v0.2-exit-gate.md`'s opening block, which says the
hold-out "has been executed on the reference model" and cites `872ef36dea33` and
`cf68c16c8592`. Their call counts are below the bound. The exit-gate document
belongs to AIC-19 and is not edited here.

**Owner decision:** keep the hold-out and disclose these attempts in the v0.2
result, or replace the two hold-out scenarios. This is needed before the final
hold-out, not before calibration.
